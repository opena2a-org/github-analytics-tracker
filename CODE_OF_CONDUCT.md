# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Summary

Be respectful. Disagree about ideas, not people. No harassment, no personal attacks, no discrimination.

## Reporting

If someone is violating these standards, email **info@opena2a.org**. We will respond within 72 hours and will not disclose your identity to the reported party without your consent.

## Enforcement

Violations may result in a warning, temporary ban from the project, or permanent removal of contribution rights, depending on severity.
